        Train your wake word

        Teach it your voice saying “Hey Oracle”. Your recordings and the model stay on this Mac, in sensors/wake; the wake word stops listening while you record or train here.

        1. Say “Hey Oracle”. Click, then say it once right away. 20 or more times is best: nearer and farther, softer and louder, the way you'd really say it.
        2. Talk about anything else. 6 seconds of ordinary talk, or of your room as it usually sounds, so it learns what not to wake on. 5 or more is good; in some, use “Oracle” in a sentence.
        3. Train. About 2 minutes the first time, under a minute after that. It goes on if you close this window, and you'll hear how it went. The wake word then listens with the new model.
        4. Try it. Click, then say “Hey Oracle”, or something else, within 3 seconds.
